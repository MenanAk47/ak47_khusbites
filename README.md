## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🌿 Advanced 7-Stage Cultivation System

* **Multi-Stage Plant Progression:** Plants progress naturally across 7 distinct visual growth stages from seed pot to fully mature cannabis plants.
* **Plant Care Mechanics:** Plants occasionally encounter growth issues requiring workers to apply fertilizer or insect spray to keep them growing healthy.
* **Harvesting Rewards:** Harvest mature crops to obtain raw cannabis leaves ready for packing, processing, or direct use.
* **Persistent Growth States:** Plant states, timers, and health automatically persist across server restarts via server-side datastores.

### 💨 Interactive Hookah & Shisha Lounge

* **Synced Physical Hose Mechanics:** Dynamic ropes connect the hookah pipe directly to the player's hand with full multi-player network sync.
* **Custom Smoking & Inhale FX:** Synchronized realistic mouth-smoke particle effects, facial smoking animations, and custom 3D inhale/exhale sound effects.
* **Puff & Session Management:** Configurable puffs per session with session fees that automatically deposit into the business society fund.
* **Refill Requirement:** Employees must periodically refill hookah units using water and harvested leaves to maintain customer availability.

### 🍪 Edible Display & Live Counter Dining

* **Interactive Edible Counter:** Displayed edibles (Kush Krisps, Herbal Delight Gummies, Blaze Bites, Chronic Crunch Cookies, Hightime Brownies) can be purchased to-go or eaten on the spot.
* **Custom Props & Plate Models:** Includes dedicated custom 3D edible props with serving plates and realistic eating animations.
* **Status & Buff System:** Consuming edibles restores hunger and thirst, relieves stress, provides armor boosts, and applies customizable drunk visual camera effects.

### 💼 Employee Shop Management & Workstation Processing

* **Modern Touchscreen Tablet UI:** Clean, responsive catalog interface categorized into Flavors, Backwoods, Joints, Vapes, Utilities, and Edibles.
* **Dynamic Shop Economy:** Authorized employees can change retail prices and stock/unstock items directly through the in-game management menu.
* **Crafting & Rolling Workstation:** Transform raw materials into custom branded joints, backwood wraps, vapor essences, and specialty strains.
* **Integrated Society Banking:** All retail purchases, edible counter dining, and hookah rentals directly generate business society revenue.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Stress, Durability & Society Integration:** Built-in support for stress relief, society accounts (esx_society, qb-management, qbx_management), inventory weight/capacity checks, and automatic datastore saving.
